## Editorial decisions

- Home and Research follow the owner's previously discussed four research axes and Processing → Quality → Data → AI → Field framework.
- General research descriptions are newly edited prose; current project summaries use the published source.
- Education describes the educational approach and existing public student activities; no course roster or enrollment details are invented.
- No unpublished experiments, numerical results, student biographies or recruitment deadlines are added.
- Contact uses the campus street address and the source English address. The unusual Korean administrative-region label in the source is omitted pending independent verification.
- Photos are original public images downloaded from the owner's Google Sites. The SVG research diagram is a concept illustration, not a data figure.
- The newest source paper is marked In press in the original listing; the site does not claim the list is a live bibliography. Update the citation after bibliographic verification.
